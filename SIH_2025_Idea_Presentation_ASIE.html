FEASIBILITY AND VIABILITY
SIH
2025
TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY
  • STM32 Controller: 168 MHz ARM Cortex-M4 handles real-time DSP
  • Hardware Timers: TIM2_TRGO provides deterministic sample clock
  • DMA Transfer: Circular buffer streams samples without CPU load
  • 12-Bit DAC: High-fidelity physical analog signal output
RISKS & CHALLENGES
  • High Sampling Rates: DAC buffer underruns at high frequencies
  • Acoustic Attenuation: Severe scattering in murky waters
  • Analog Distortion: Amplifier non-linearity & noise
  • Power Limits: AUV battery depletion on long pings
MITIGATION STRATEGIES
  • Precomputed Sine LUTs: Fast lookup table waveform synthesis
  • Digital Windowing: Suppresses spectral sidelobes & harmonics
  • Hardware Low-Pass Filter: Smooths staircase DAC output
  • Battery Eco Mode: Auto-scales amplitude when battery < 25%
VIABILITY & SCALABILITY
  • Modular Payload: Integrates into standard AUV sensor bays
  • Software-Defined: Flash-upgradeable waveform library
  • Low Power: 82% CPU power savings via DMA streaming
  • Digital Twin: In-situ real-time signal validation
